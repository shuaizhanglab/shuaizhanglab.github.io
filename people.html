Principal Investigator

Shuai Zhang

Associate Professor

Dr. Zhang received his B.S. in Materials Physics from Dalian University of Technology in 2011. He subsequently earned his Ph.D. in Condensed Matter Physics from Fudan University under the mentorship of Prof. Shiwei Wu in 2017. His doctoral research centered on the development and application of a cryogen-free low-temperature scanning tunneling microscope. Following his Ph.D., he worked as a Xide Postdoctoral Fellow in the Microscopy Lab of Surfaces and Nanostructures at Fudan University, where he investigated pump-probe STM techniques and electron cascade tunneling behaviors in heterostructures.

From 2018 to 2025, Dr. Zhang joined Prof. Dmitri Basov's Infrared Nano-optics of Quantum Materials Laboratory at Columbia University, serving first as a Postdoctoral Research Scientist and later as an Associate Research Scientist. During his tenure at Columbia, he played a leading role in designing and building a cryogenic scattering-type scanning near-field optical microscope. His work extensively involved near-field infrared and THz spectroscopy, probing the strong coupling of light with elementary excitations in solids. In June 2025, Dr. Zhang joined the faculty at Fudan University to establish his independent research group.
